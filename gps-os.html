GPS OS

Grid reference · Great Britain and Ireland

GPS OS provides a fast and accurate way to locate your position on an Ordnance Survey map, both in Great Britain and Ireland.

View on the App Store

Features

  • No phone signal required — works in the most remote locations.
  • British or Irish grid, as 6, 8 or 10 figure grid references.
  • OSTN15 transform data for high conversion accuracy (better than 1 m).
  • A large, clear, high contrast display ensures you can easily read your position in any light conditions.
  • Shows elevation and position accuracy, in your choice of units.
  • Magnetic compass.
  • Copy or share your grid reference with other apps or people.
  • Apple Watch app showing your grid reference and its accuracy.
  • GPS runs only while the app is open, never in the background.
  • No adverts, no tracking.

Waypoints

An annual Premium subscription adds waypoints:

  • Save locations from the map, your current location, or by entering a British or Irish grid reference.
  • See the distance and direction to any saved location.
  • Mark each location with one of 30 icons.
  • View your locations on a map or in a list.
  • Open a location in Apple Maps.
  • Share locations with other apps or people, or export them all as a GPX file.
  • Deleted locations stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Your locations are stored only on your iPhone.
